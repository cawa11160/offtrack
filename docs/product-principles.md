# Offtrack Product Principles

## Motto

Musicians first.

Offtrack exists to help musicians get discovered, understand their audience, and convert attention into durable fan relationships. Listener features matter because they create demand, trust, and repeat discovery, but the product should not become a generic streaming clone.

## Product Positioning

Offtrack is a musician-first discovery platform:

- Musicians can publish tracks they own or control.
- Listeners can discover music through recommendations, search, and artist surfaces.
- Every meaningful listener action should feed back into artist insight.
- Artist outcomes should connect to concrete next steps: follows, external streams, merch, concerts, mailing lists, or direct sales.

## Decision Rules

- Prefer features that make musicians easier to discover, understand, and support.
- Treat playback, recommendations, search, and profiles as part of one discovery loop.
- Do not expand merch, concerts, billing, or social features unless they improve musician conversion.
- Do not imply full-catalog streaming rights unless licensing and takedown workflows support that claim.
- Favor small, measurable launches over broad unfinished product surfaces.

## Core Loop

1. A musician creates or claims an artist account.
2. The musician uploads a track and completes profile metadata.
3. Offtrack recommends that track to likely listeners.
4. Listeners play, like, save, share, or click through.
5. The musician sees what worked.
6. The musician improves the release, profile, or campaign.

## North Star

Qualified musician-listener connections per uploaded track.

A connection is qualified when a listener takes a meaningful action after discovering a musician through Offtrack, such as a full play, like, save, follow, profile open, merch click, concert click, Spotify click, or direct fan signup.
